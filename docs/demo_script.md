# 3-minute demo script (no images of victims)

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:30 | Title, map of the Trishuli / Bhote Koshi valley | 26 Aug 2026 flash flood in Rasuwa. Question: where did the ground change, what is exposed, who lost road access to a hospital, from free satellite data only. |
| 0:30-1:30 | Terminal: one run on a judge-style area and date | Same-track Sentinel-1 pair chosen automatically, pre-event OSM snapshot, detector, damage overlay, cut-off. Point at the status line and the class-null table (event vs four no-flood nulls, rule > 3x). |
| 1:30-2:15 | Dashboard: toggle detected change, low/high scenarios, buildings, roads, bridges; click a cut-off settlement and its first-break marker | Every number in the panel is read from the JSON outputs; ranges come from thresholds, mask +/-1 px and the bridge policy. |
| 2:15-2:45 | Limitations slide / one-page sitrep | Single track; valley floor only (the unknown share is shown); no cause attribution; S2 excluded; AI model sees open water only; EMSR927 scores landslides, not flood extent. |
| 2:45-3:00 | Attribution footer | The three required data credits and Sen1Floods11. |

EMS credit: European Union, Copernicus Emergency Management Service data (EMSR927, used for checking only).
